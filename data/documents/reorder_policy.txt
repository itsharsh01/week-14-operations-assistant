Reorder Policy — Small Electronics Store

When current stock is at or below the reorder level for a product, place a reorder immediately.

Standard reorder quantity: bring stock up to 2× the reorder level (minimum one case pack where applicable).

Low-stock items are flagged daily. Operations must review the inventory CSV and open a purchase request within 1 business day.

Priority: products more than 50% below reorder level are urgent (e.g. stock 5 vs level 15).
